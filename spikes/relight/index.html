ADR 0018 step 2 · relighting spike

Keys and notes

←→ ±10 min · Space play · [] front fill ±5% · T translucency map · I large sprites · H hide this panel. Add ?compare=1 for the keyframed comparison (1 2 3 X N).

The Sun and Moon light the scene from their real direction, so a southern Sun backlights it. Translucency lets light through grass, petals, and leaves lit from behind. Front fill stands in for light bounced back from the field: the same Sun and Moon mirrored to the viewer's side, at this fraction of their power, on the hill and the plants only, never the mountains.

Keyframes are authored once, on the March equinox (), and know nothing of the season or where the Sun is; the relit half uses the real Sun and Moon. Sky, stars, Sun, Moon, and ground shadows are the same code in both halves; only the shading of the art differs.
